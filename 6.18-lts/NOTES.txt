linux-m8x/debian-latest/debian/patches/001-*.patch through 098-*.patch are
adapted from xdarklight's linux fork, branch
"meson-mx-integration-6.20-20251223":
https://github.com/xdarklight/linux/blob/meson-mx-integration-6.20-20251223/

They were originally carried as a single squashed file
(01-xdarklight-linux-meson-mx-integration-6.20-20251223.patch) and have
since been split into one file per upstream commit, in their original
order, for easier review and bisection. Some of them are marked by
upstream as WiP, HACK, TODO or DO NOT USE (visible in the filename
suffix) -- review those before relying on them.

linux-m8x/debian-latest/debian/patches/099-*.patch onward are local
fixes/additions on top of the xdarklight-derived series, adapting it to
vanilla kernel 6.18.38.

Patches are tracked in debian/patches/series (quilt format) and are
applied both by build.sh (manual build) and by dpkg-source/dpkg-buildpackage
when building the linux-m8x Debian package from linux-m8x/debian-latest.
